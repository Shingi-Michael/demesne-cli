# Design guide

Rules every mockup in this project follows, on top of Demesne's own. They are Mike's, in his words where he gave them. One per line; edit freely, or say a new one in a chat and it is added here.

## Rules

- Minimal surface: fold a new fact into an existing row, chip or tooltip before adding anything new.
- No menus and no side panels. The cards on the right are the one exception, and they must never be the focus.
- A list always has demarcation: rows with hairline dividers in one rounded box, like the app's own rows.
- Blue stays the accent. Pink only marks where you are (and the release line); never pink in place of blue.
- The Mac's own red, yellow and green window buttons stay; never draw custom ones.
- Text never sits on top of a drawing.
- Foundry is a place to look: no buttons or controls on a Foundry page for the person; they answer in the chat.
- Things line up: symmetry and shared edges first, off-centre only on purpose.
- Show new ideas, not tweaks of what is there, and one feature at a time.
- Several options always name a recommended one and say why.
